    How these figures are worked out
    • Cost per unit is the total paid for tokens divided by the units received, so it already includes levies and taxes.
    • Daily use comes from your meter readings. On a prepaid meter: units left at the first reading, plus tokens loaded since, minus units left at the last reading, divided by the days between.
    • Appliance use is an estimate: watts × number × hours per day × days per week ÷ 7 ÷ 1,000.
    • Not explained by your list is the difference between what the meter measured and what your appliance list adds up to.
    • Savings assume typical reductions for each habit change, priced at your own cost per unit.
